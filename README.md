# Customer Loyalty, Segmentation & Sentiment Analysis
Predicting customer loyalty, segmenting customers, and analysing review sentiment to inform data-driven marketing decisions.


This project analyses customer behaviour and reviews for a global games retailer to improve sales and marketing strategy. Decision tree modelling was used to identify the key drivers of customer loyalty points, while K-means clustering revealed distinct customer segments based on spending and income. Sentiment analysis of customer reviews using TextBlob and VADER showed predominantly positive feedback and highlighted products for promotion or improvement. The project demonstrates end-to-end data analysis, from exploratory analysis to actionable business insights.
